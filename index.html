I work as a distributed systems expert at ModelBest. I architect and build AI infrastructure, delivering efficient, reliable, and observable systems. I also work on systems that can improve themselves over time.

More background+

Previously, I was a researcher at HKRC, focusing on reliability in hyperscale systems, self-evolving AI infrastructure, and software design for coding agents. I received my Ph.D. in Computer Science from HKBU, supervised by Prof. Xiaowen Chu and Dr. Amelie Chi Zhou. I was also a visiting student at NUS, advised by Prof. Bingsheng He. I received my B.Eng. in Electrical Engineering from HUST.
